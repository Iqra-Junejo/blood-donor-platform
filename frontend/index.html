Why donate

Small act. Huge impact.

Blood cannot be manufactured. It can only come from generous people like you.

Save up to 3 lives

Safe and simple

The process takes under an hour and uses sterile, single-use equipment every time.

Strengthen your community

Be the reason a family gets good news. Patients nearby need blood every single day.
